🔄 2. End-to-End Data Pipeline: From Physical Paper to Live ERP

Here is what happens technically behind the scenes when a physical invoice or Delivery Challan is processed:

Stage What Happens Technically Technology Used Output / Result
1. Physical Scan Inward Vendor bills, Delivery Challans, and QC certificates are scanned via flatbed scanner to PDF. Physical Scanner / Multi-Page PDF Raw raster PDF placed in company scan archive.
2. Multimodal AI Extraction PDF pages are rendered into high-resolution buffers and passed to Google Gemini Flash Vision LLM. Google Gemini Flash Vision API Structured JSON with vendor, date, rate, HSN, tax, heat numbers.
3. RAG Verification The RAG engine matches extracted supplier names against the official Vendor Master (`Sri Murugan`, `Everbright`, `Unitherm`). Retrieval-Augmented Generation / SQL Entity-linked line items; zero hallucination or spelling mismatch.
4. Database Commit Validated rows are written in an atomic transaction to PostgreSQL tables (`documents`, `document_line_items`). PostgreSQL Relational DB (AWS Mumbai) Persistent storage with 100% audit trail linking to original PDF.
5. Live Dashboard Display Next.js frontend pulls live queries to show total tonnage, subcontractor ledger, tool wear, and costs. Next.js 15 / Tailwind / Vercel Edge Instant visibility for Managing Director on mobile or laptop.

🎯 4. Admin & IT Department Q&A Cheat Sheet

When colleagues from your Admin, IT, or Accounts department ask technical questions, use these clear answers:

Q1: "Where is our application running? Do we have a server inside our factory?"
Answer: "No, we don't have a physical server in our factory that requires AC, UPS batteries, or maintenance. Our application is hosted on Vercel Serverless Cloud with a global Content Delivery Network, and our database is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in Mumbai. This guarantees 99.99% uptime and zero maintenance headaches."
Q2: "What database are we using and where is our company data stored?"
Answer: "We are using PostgreSQL 15, an enterprise-grade relational database managed by Supabase on AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1). Because it is physically hosted in Mumbai, India, our queries load in under 25 milliseconds, and our data complies fully with Indian data residency laws."
Q3: "How is AI involved? Isn't AI known to make things up (hallucinate)?"
Answer: "We use Google Gemini Flash Vision LLM strictly for document reading (OCR), combined with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). The AI does not guess numbers; it only reads what is visually printed on our scanned bills and delivery challans, cross-verifies against our verified vendor list, and links every single number directly to the original PDF scan."
Q4: "Are our confidential vendor rates and invoices exposed to the public?"
Answer: "Absolutely not. All database tables are protected with Row-Level Security (RLS) and encrypted login tokens (JWT). Our Google AI API calls are strictly enterprise-level; Google's API policy guarantees that customer data is never used to train public AI models."
Q5: "What happens if our internet goes down at the factory?"
Answer: "The web application is hosted in the cloud, so the Managing Director and management can access it from their mobile phones, home laptops, or any office PC anytime. Once factory internet resumes, local entries sync immediately."
Q6: "How does the system track our manufacturing cycle?"
Answer: "The system models our exact 8-step production pipeline: Raw Material (NG Sales) ➔ Cutting 780mm (Sri Murugan) ➔ CNC Turning (4 vendors) ➔ Bore QC & Tapping ➔ Slitting ➔ Heat Treatment (Unitherm) ➔ 100% Load Testing (190kN) ➔ Packing & Dispatch to Chennai Central Store. It automatically balances the tons of steel sent out against the finished pieces received."
